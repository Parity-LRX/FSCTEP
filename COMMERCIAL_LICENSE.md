non-commercial academic research and teaching. The full [LICENSE](LICENSE)

## Free academic use

Students, educators, and researchers at nonprofit educational or research
institutions and public research institutions may use the Software free of
charge solely for the non-commercial academic purposes defined in LICENSE.
Ordinary tuition, public or charitable research grants, and publication in
fee-charging journals do not alone make an otherwise eligible use commercial.
Academic affiliation alone does not exempt a commercial project.

## A separate paid commercial license is required

The academic license does not authorize corporate internal R&D or evaluation,
commercial product development, paid consulting, research for commercial
clients, commercial hosted services, or training models for commercial use.
Offering access free of charge does not make a business use academic.

Before beginning such use, contact the repository owner
[Ruixiang Lin / Parity-LRX](https://github.com/Parity-LRX) through a licensing
inquiry in this repository's Issues. Describe your organization, intended use,
deployment scope, and whether distribution or hosted services are planned.
Do not include confidential business details in a public issue; request a
private contact channel if needed.

Commercial use requires a separate written agreement and payment of the fees
agreed in that agreement. An inquiry, download, or payment by itself grants
no license. Pricing and availability are determined by the copyright holder;
this repository does not promise approval or publish a standard price.
